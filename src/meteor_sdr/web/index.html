MANUAL REVIEW GUIDE · ILLUSTRATIONS

What to look for in the live displays

Automatic image snapshots are off. Watch the live Echo Power trace and waterfall, then use these shapes only as visual clues—not confirmation.

SHORT-TRAIL CANDIDATE

Fast rise and decay, often only a fraction of a second.

LONG-TRAIL CANDIDATE

Lasts seconds or longer and may fade irregularly.

LIKELY INTERFERENCE

Repeating spikes, persistent reception, or the whole band rising together.

What is actually detected?

A distant broadcast may reflect from the ionized trail left as a meteoroid ablates high in the atmosphere. This cannot locate a meteorite or prove an impact.
