FIELD GUIDE

What a meteor may look like

Underdense echo

A thin, bright vertical dash near your watched frequency, often lasting less than a second.

Overdense echo

A brighter, longer, irregular trace that can bend, split, or fade over several seconds.

Probably interference

A full-width horizontal flash, a signal that never leaves, or a perfectly repeating pattern.

Automatic saves are candidates, not confirmed meteors. The strongest evidence is a brief reflection on a normally absent distant station.
