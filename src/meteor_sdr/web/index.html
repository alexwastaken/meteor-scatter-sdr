SIGNATURE GUIDE

Reading the waterfall

UNDERDENSE

Brief, thin vertical trace near the watched frequency.

OVERDENSE

Longer bright trace that bends, splits, or fades irregularly.

INTERFERENCE

Full-width flash, permanent carrier, or perfectly repeating pattern.

The detector waits for a multi-frame rise and decay and rejects band-wide flashes. Saved events remain unconfirmed candidates, not proof of a meteor.
